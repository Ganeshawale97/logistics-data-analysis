# Logistics Metrics Framework

## Core KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Management use |
|---|---|---|
| On-Time Delivery Rate | On-time shipments / total eligible shipments | Service reliability |
| Average Delivery Time | Mean delivery duration | Fulfillment speed |
| Cost per Shipment | Total transport cost / shipments | Cost control |
| Cost per Kilometer | Transport cost / route distance | Route efficiency |
| Shipment Volume | Total shipped quantity | Capacity planning |
| Inventory Turnover | Cost of goods sold / average inventory | Inventory efficiency |
| Stockout Rate | Stockout events / demand opportunities | Availability |

## KPI design principles

1. Define the numerator and denominator explicitly.
2. Specify the reporting period.
3. Segment by zone, route or transport mode when useful.
4. Compare against a baseline or target.
5. Avoid mixing cancelled/returned transactions with positive demand without documenting the treatment.
6. Pair service KPIs with cost KPIs to prevent one-dimensional optimization.

## Example decision

If on-time delivery falls while cost per shipment rises, management should investigate route congestion, capacity utilization, dispatch timing and transport-mode mix rather than changing one variable in isolation.
